National Health Care Policy Review Survey

Horizon EC is gathering first-hand accounts of everyday work in healthcare from the people who do it: clinicians, nurses, technicians, administrative, billing, legal and IT staff.

Your answers describe how care is actually delivered, where the system helps and where it gets in the way. Write as much or as little as you like. Plain, specific answers are the most useful.

  • Anonymous. No name, email or IP address is recorded.
  • Every question except your role is optional.
  • About 15 minutes if you answer everything.
  • Please leave out patient names and identifying details.
